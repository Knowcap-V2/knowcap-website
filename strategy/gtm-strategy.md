# Go-to-Market Strategy

Two paid acquisition channels in the launch phase, with a third strategic path being debated.

## Primary Channels

### 1. Meta paid (Facebook + Instagram)
Run via StratDev agency. 90-day discovery sprint, $150–200/day spend, target sub-$50 CAC. Full plan in [`campaigns/meta-paid-sprint.md`](../campaigns/meta-paid-sprint.md).

### 2. LinkedIn outbound
Run via Heyreach automation + Sales Navigator. ~4,500 messages/month across 3 accounts, target 20–25 booked calls/month. Full plan in [`campaigns/linkedin-outbound.md`](../campaigns/linkedin-outbound.md).

## The Strategic Debate (Unresolved)

| Path | Pros | Cons |
|---|---|---|
| **Generic horizontal SaaS** | Scales fast, no integration dependency, broad TAM | Commodity risk — many AI note-takers in market |
| **Odoo vertical specialist** | Defensible moat (200K+ Odoo partners, ~6-month competitive window), productivity-sharing pricing possible | Narrower TAM, slower expansion |

Hassan's leaning has shifted multiple times. Current approach: run both for 90 days, let CAC and conversion data decide.

## SEO (Deferred)

Long-tail keywords identified ("remote team monitoring", "train remote employees", "meeting productivity tools") + AI SEO (LLM visibility optimization). Not started until paid media shows proof of concept (~6 months from launch).

## Pricing Tiers

Free / Basic / Pro / Business / Enterprise. Productivity-sharing model proposed for vertical Odoo path — billed against productivity gains rather than seats.
