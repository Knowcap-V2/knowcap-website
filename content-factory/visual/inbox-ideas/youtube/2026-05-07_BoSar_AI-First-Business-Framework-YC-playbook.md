## Light synthesis (3-5 bullets)

- Thesis from Bogdan, riffing on a recent YC talk + Jack Dorsey at Block: AI should not be a tool a company *uses* — it should be the **operating system the company runs on**. Companies that flip first compound advantage daily.
- The framework is 4 steps: **Learn → Wire → Automate → Scale.**
  - **Learn (1–2 weeks):** team gets daily reps in Claude Code (terminal/IDE, full power, founder tier) or Claude Co-work (desktop app, plugin sandbox, team-friendly). Goal is the "mind-blowing moment" that builds conviction.
  - **Wire:** build the *business brain* — make the company queryable to AI. Structured CLAUDE.md files + Obsidian knowledge base + live data sources (sales transcripts, Slack, CRM, Stripe) auto-flowing in. Compounds over time, not a one-time setup.
  - **Automate:** for each department (marketing / sales / delivery / ops) build agents with **skills** (= SOPs for AI). Each skill is a closed loop with a **test harness** — define what good looks like, AI iterates until it meets the bar, you review output not process.
  - **Scale:** add new departments / skills, not headcount. "Maximize tokens, not people."
- Why startups + small businesses win: large companies are cruise ships (legacy systems, retraining thousands, change-aversion). A solo or small team is a speedboat — can re-architect from scratch today. YC quote: "operate a thousand times faster than incumbents." 95% of businesses haven't restructured ops around AI yet — that gap is the opportunity.
- Old rule / new rule list (the punchy closer):
  - Come to meetings with ideas → **come with working prototypes**
  - Hire to execute → **build skills to execute, hire to manage AI departments**
  - Scale by headcount → **scale by departments + skills**
  - Knowledge in heads + Confluence + Drive → **knowledge in the business brain**
  - Manually approve everything → **define test harnesses, AI self-checks, you review outputs**
  - Middle managers route info → **the intelligence layer routes info**
  - Speed limited by team availability → **speed limited only by how many skills you build**
- Pitch close: AI First Academy + done-for-you implementations.
