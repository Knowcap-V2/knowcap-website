## Core thesis

- AI as **operating system**, not tool
- Tools plateau; methodology compounds
- "6 months from now, the AI running your business will know more about your operations than most employees"
- Right person + right AI system = output of an entire team

## 4 key concepts (the substrate of his pitch)

### 1. Closed loops vs open loops
Borrowed from control-systems engineering. Most businesses run **open loop**: decide → execute → maybe check. **Closed loop**: continuous self-monitoring + correction (thermostat metaphor). Sales example:
- Every call auto-transcribed → AI analyzes objections → next call prep auto-built → follow-ups based on actual discussion (not templates) → CRM auto-updated → system learns best patterns over time

This is **literally the Knowcap loop** described in the content-strategy-vision doc.

### 2. Business brain (= YC's "company brain")
The biggest blocker to AI automation is NOT the models — it's that company knowledge is **scattered**: heads, Slack, Drive, email, support tickets, databases. AI agents can't operate on fragmented knowledge.

Solution: a structured layer that captures pricing logic, team structure, client data, processes, goals — and **updates itself automatically**.

YC quote: *"Every company in the world is going to need one of these."*

### 3. Test harnesses
Define what "good" looks like → AI self-checks against your standards before output reaches you. Humans review **outputs, not process**.

### 4. The new org chart
- Skills replace SOPs
- Humans manage AI departments instead of executing tasks
- Scale by adding skills, not headcount ("**token-maxing**")
