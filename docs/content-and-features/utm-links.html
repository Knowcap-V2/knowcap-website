Knowcap UTM Links

Copy-paste attribution set — never share a bare knowcap.ai link again.

Born from the Jun 2026 lost-leads incident: both /beta submitters showed as "direct" — untraceable. PostHog and GA4 auto-capture UTM parameters; no site change needed.

The rule: every link you post or DM carries utm_source (where) + utm_medium (how) + utm_campaign (which push). Attribution then shows up automatically in PostHog (session entry + person profile) and GA4 (Traffic acquisition).

Formula for any URL

https://knowcap.ai/<path>?utm_source=<where>&utm_medium=<how>&utm_campaign=<push-name>
  • utm_source — the platform: linkedin x instagram whatsapp telegram email newsletter youtube producthunt
  • utm_medium — the mechanism: social dm email video signature paid
  • utm_campaign — the specific push, kebab-case + date when time-bound: beta-push-jul26, launch-week
  • All lowercase, hyphens not spaces. Blog posts too: https://knowcap.ai/blog/<slug>?utm_source=linkedin&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=blog-share

Where attribution shows up

  • PostHog (project 441003): person profile → initial UTM properties; session entry props on every session. Combined with beta_contact_entered / beta_form_submitted events, every lead now arrives with source + email attached.
  • GA4: Reports → Acquisition → Traffic acquisition (session source/medium/campaign).
Don't add UTMs to internal links (page-to-page on knowcap.ai) — it overwrites the real source mid-session. External shares only.
