3 things the audit surfaced beyond the nav

1. /book contradicts itself. You said it's send-on-request only — but it's the most-linked private URL on the site: the footer, both CTAs on all 5 compare pages, homepage CTAs, and the sitemap. Gating and these links can't both be true. Fix: pull every public link; route demo requests through Contact, you send /book manually. (Compare-page CTAs become "Contact us".)
2. /a /b /c /d are duplicate homepages in your sitemap with no canonical. Four near-copies of the homepage submitted to Google competing with / for its own keywords. Fix: remove from sitemap + add a canonical to / (or noindex).
3. The 5 compare pages are 5 islands. No /compare hub, no cross-links between them — the weakest structure for a "knowcap vs X" keyword cluster. Fix: build a /compare index + sibling links (hub-and-spoke). Bonus: a "Latest writing" block on the homepage linking the 3 newest posts.

Your questions, answered

Is there a way to reach the blog from the main page?
No — that's the core defect. Fix: Blog in the top nav + footer.
What should be in the top menu?
Keep it lean + conversion-first. How it works · For your agents · FAQ · Blog · Contact + Log in + the one Get Started Free button. Add only Blog.
What should be in the footer?
A 4-column sitemap: Product · Compare · Company (incl. Blog) · Get started. This is where the orphans get homes.
Leave as-is, or change?
Change. The blog/compare/vertical pages are invisible to both visitors and crawlers right now.
Blog in the footer or the top?
Both. Top nav = answers your complaint + feeds crawl equity; footer = catch-all. A plain text link, so it never competes with the CTA.
Pages with no hyperlinks (like /book)?
Right call to gate them — but make it consistent. /book is currently linked everywhere; pull those links so "send-on-request" is actually true. /invest, /partner, /vendor, /offer are already correctly unlinked.
