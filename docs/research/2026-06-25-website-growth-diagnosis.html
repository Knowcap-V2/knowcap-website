Why the blogs don't pull — 4 root causes

#Root causeThe evidence
1Zero domain authorityNew domain, ~31 referral sessions, almost no backlinks. You cannot rank for competitive terms no matter how good the content is. This is the real SEO gate — content is not the lever, links are.
2Language mismatchThe demand is Arabic — اودو 8,100 · محضر اجتماع 1,300 · تدقيق 480 /mo. The blogs are English long-tail (~210/mo at best). English pages will never rank for Arabic queries.
3Brand-namespace collisionThe 325 non-brand impressions are confusion noise — "cap ai", "ai cap", "cloudcap ai", "infocap", "nocap ai". People hunting some other "*cap ai" product. Not topic intent. Almost no impressions for real ICP topics.
4Wrong channel betYour own positioning research already said it: "awareness-led inbound is weak — outbound and partner-channel is required." The data confirms it. SEO was the wrong primary engine for this ICP at this stage.
Hidden bright spot: your /compare/* pages (knowcap-vs-otter, -read-ai, -granola, -fireflies, -fellow) rank page-1, positions 3–9 — but get 0 clicks. They're ranking; the titles/snippets just aren't earning the click. Cheapest fix on the board.

Move 1 — Stop digging (this week)

  • Kill the 3-EN-posts/week burn cadence. It manufactures pages that rank for nothing. A missed post beats another asset into the void.
  • Re-aim the blogger loop: from "SEO acquisition" → "ammunition for outbound + AI-citation." Same engine, different job.
  • Publish Arabic on the real-volume keywords (اودو, محضر اجتماع, تدقيق). Flip the EN-only rule — it's the single biggest self-inflicted cap.

Move 2 — Fix the foundation (this month) — content isn't the lever, these are

  • Backlinks / domain authority ← THE SEO gate. Odoo Partner Portal listing, SMEtools cross-links, Odoo community posts, 2–3 guest posts, directory listings. Without links nothing ranks.
  • CTR repair on the pages that already rank — rewrite /compare/* titles + meta to earn the click (pos 3–9 → actual traffic). Hours of work, immediate payoff.
  • GEO / AI-citation — the AI-Assistant channel (7 sessions, growing) is the realistic inbound for a new domain. Add llms.txt, keep citable passages, get into "best Odoo / meeting tool" roundups. Your blogs are already GEO-structured — lean in.
  • Arabic content (from Move 1) compounds here.

Move 3 — Fuel what works now (this quarter) — outbound + partner

  • Direct = 64% of traffic. Your GTM is founder-led + partner-channel (the documented moat). Lean in: LinkedIn outbound to Odoo partners (SMEtools network + Odoo Partner Portal), partner co-marketing / revenue-share.
  • Content feeds outbound — every post becomes a DM asset + a partner-share asset, not an SEO bet.
  • Capture the Direct traffic you already get: the /beta signup (just shipped) + a sharper CTA on /, where 58 of 97 organic sessions land.

The reframe

SEO is a 6–12 month compounding bet that needs links + Arabic first. It is not where this quarter's traffic comes from.

This quarter's traffic = outbound + network + AI-citation. Re-point the content engine to feed those, fix the SEO foundation in the background, and stop grading a 3-week-old SEO bet as if it should already pay. The blog engine isn't broken — it was aimed at the wrong target.
