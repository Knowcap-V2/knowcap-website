# The 5 MENA Business YouTube Channels Every Odoo Partner Should Know

When the Knowcap MENA SME Research, June 2026, mined YouTube comments across business and ERP content channels, five names came up repeatedly — both as the channels MENA Odoo professionals subscribe to and as the places where the working language of MENA ERP delivery is being shaped in real time. These channels are not equally famous outside the region. Inside it, they are the names every Odoo implementation Project Manager, every Saudi accounting consultant, every Cairo ERP developer knows. The list is not academic. It is the channels that show up in our research dataset over and over with high comment engagement, real audience questions, and substantive technical answers. If you sell into the MENA Odoo partner ecosystem and you are not familiar with these names, you are missing the conversation.

## Yehia Tech (يحيى تك)

Yehia Tech is the Arabic-speaking Egyptian channel that anchors a meaningful share of the MENA Odoo developer learning journey. Comments under Yehia's Odoo content in our dataset include genuine learning questions ("هل ممكن تعطينا مصادر تعلم odoo و sap؟" — "can you give us learning resources for Odoo and SAP"), real working stories ("اشتغلت مطور odoo لمدة 3 اشهر بعدين الشركة مشتني" — "I worked as an Odoo developer for 3 months and then the company let me go"), and substantive technical corrections from peer developers debating Odoo's REST API capabilities. The audience is Egyptian, Saudi, and Gulf-based Odoo developers in the early to mid-career range. The Knowcap MENA SME Research, June 2026, identifies Yehia Tech as the single highest-engagement Arabic-language Odoo education channel in MENA. The channel is at [youtube.com/@YehiaTech](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=Yehia+Tech+يحيى+تك).

## ODOO IT YOURSELF

ODOO IT YOURSELF is the English-language channel that captures the global Odoo self-implementer audience, with a meaningful MENA viewer base. The most-engaged comments in our dataset under this channel's content reveal the audience pain clearly: "End user here. Been trying to learn Odoo for 7 months now. I had NO idea it would be this impossibly difficult." Another: "Your videos are super valuable, thank you! It would be great to hear some advice for aspiring self starters wanting to get into the Odoo dev game. What to look out for and risks and opportunities." This is the audience MENA Odoo partners compete with for client mindshare — clients who tried to self-implement, hit the wall, and are now ready to pay a partner. The channel is at [odooityourself.com](https://www.odooityourself.com) with YouTube presence at [youtube.com/@ODOOITYOURSELF](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=ODOO+IT+YOURSELF).

## E-Accounting & ERP

E-Accounting & ERP is a regionally focused channel anchored in the Egypt and Gulf accounting-professional audience. Comments under their Odoo content in our research include practical implementation questions ("سؤال لو سمحت هل يمكن ربط نظام كامتند الخاص بالحضور والانصراف عن طريق الجوال بنظام أودو؟" — asking about integrating a mobile attendance system with Odoo) and direct learning requests ("محتاجة اتعلم odoo sales"). The audience leans toward in-practice accountants and finance team members who are evaluating or supporting Odoo deployments inside their organisations, not the developer audience that Yehia Tech captures. For MENA Odoo partners, this is where the buyer-influencer audience lives — the controllers, the heads of finance, the accounting team leads who recommend the ERP choice to their leadership. The Knowcap MENA SME Research, June 2026, found this channel to be the strongest accounting-buyer touch point in Arabic.

## Software Connect

Software Connect is the international software comparison channel that runs Odoo-vs-Zoho, Odoo-vs-Salesforce, and similar comparison videos with high engagement from MENA viewers. The standout comment under their Odoo-vs-Zoho video, with 105 likes, captures the regional buyer's framing perfectly: "As a businessman I would prefer Zoho, as a developer I would love to choose Odoo. Odoo gives me freedom to create my own modules, customize things as I like, but Zoho gives me easy and fast access to tools, essential customisation only." Another with 34 likes describes a hybrid Odoo-Zoho setup with N8N connecting them — exactly the kind of MENA SME architecture that grows out of a partner not being able to deliver everything in one stack. Software Connect is at [softwareconnect.com](https://softwareconnect.com) with YouTube content under the same name.

## Arab Techno (عرب تكنو) and the broader Arabic AI-curious audience

Arab Techno (عرب تكنو) sits adjacent to the strict Odoo channels but is the one MENA Odoo partners should not ignore — because it is where the broader Arabic-speaking AI-curious audience encounters the AI tool category. Comments in our dataset include extensive engagement with ChatGPT tutorials ("Use the ChatGPT Pro plan for more precise and high-quality answers") and similar questions about AI subscription tiers. This is the channel that primes the Saudi and Egyptian buyer for the AI meeting tool category before they ever search for one. Adjacent channels in the same Arabic AI-curious tier include Rawaa (رواء) and Mohamed Medhat (محمد مدحت), which run high-engagement Arabic content about ChatGPT and Gemini specifically. The Knowcap MENA SME Research, June 2026, identifies these channels as the awareness layer that complements the Odoo-specific channels.

## FAQ

### Are these the only MENA business YouTube channels worth following?

No. The Knowcap MENA SME Research, June 2026, tracked roughly 25 MENA business and ERP channels with meaningful audience and engagement. The five named here are the ones that came up most consistently in the comment analysis specifically for Odoo and ERP content. Other notable channels include putcodes (which focuses on Odoo developer questions in Arabic), Ahmed Hassan Algammal (an Egyptian ERP educator), غريب الشيخ (Ghareeb Elshaikh — Arabic Odoo content), Waleed Maestro (Arabic Odoo tutorials), and Webkul (a multi-language Odoo services channel with global reach). The full channel list will be published as part of the Knowcap partnerships program. The five highlighted here are the best starting point for any company entering the MENA Odoo ecosystem.

### What kind of content performs best on these MENA channels?

Practical, in-depth, step-by-step content with screen recordings. The MENA Odoo audience is learning while watching — they have Odoo open in another window and they pause the video every 30 seconds. Talking-head explainers underperform. Bilingual content (Arabic narration with English code on screen, or vice versa) performs particularly well because it matches the working reality of MENA Odoo professionals, who switch languages while working. Comment engagement is highest on content that includes real client stories, real bugs, and real conflict resolution — not generic "how to use Odoo" content. The Knowcap MENA SME Research, June 2026, captured this pattern by counting comment likes against video topic categories and ranking by engagement intensity.

### Why does the Arabic-language content concentrate on Egyptian creators?

Egypt has the largest Arabic-speaking online content creator base in MENA — driven by population size (over 100 million Egyptians), strong YouTube monetization for Arabic content, and a long tradition of Egyptian Arabic being the de facto entertainment Arabic for the entire region. When a Saudi or Emirati or Moroccan viewer watches Arabic business content, the majority of what they watch is produced in Egyptian Arabic. This is the language production reality for MENA business YouTube. Khaleeji Arabic, Levantine Arabic, and Maghrebi Arabic creators exist and have meaningful audiences, but the Egyptian Arabic creators tend to anchor the major channels. The Knowcap MENA SME Research, June 2026, identified this as a relevant fact for any company doing Arabic content marketing in MENA.
